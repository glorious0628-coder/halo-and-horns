Concept sheet, one image: ornate decorative "flower objects" for a bright, kid-friendly Roblox RPG town in the clouds (Halo Citadel: white marble, warm gold trim, sky-blue and cream, soft clouds). The previous version was too plain — make these RICH and FANCY but still built from simple rounded forms. Show 8 different flower objects in a row like a catalogue, each one = a straight stem with gold rings/spiral bands + a big ornate head: layered stacked petals (2-3 rounded layers in two colors), a big glowing orb held in a gold cup with small floating mini-orbs around it, a star head with gold filigree frame and inset gem, a tulip cup with gold rim and pearl drops, a bell with gold clapper and ribbon bow, a heart inside a gold ring with sparkles, a cloud puff with tiny gold stars, a sparkle-burst with crystal shards; some with a soft glowing halo ring behind the head, hanging pearls or tiny ribbons, and small leaf pairs in mint/green with gold edges. Colors: saturated pastels (rose pink, lavender, mint, butter yellow, sky blue, peach, pearl white) with lots of warm gold accents and tiny gem highlights. Vary heights (one tall centerpiece per group). Below, smaller: one tall centerpiece flower in a round white-marble planter with gold trim, and a row of 5 along a low marble garden edge, so the owner sees them in the town. Style: clean stylized 3D render look, chunky rounded shapes, glossy heads with soft glow, NO outlines, NO cast shadows, NO ground shadow, even light-box lighting, plain very-light-cream background, no text, no people. Still simple enough to build from basic parts (cylinders, spheres, discs, stars, rings).
