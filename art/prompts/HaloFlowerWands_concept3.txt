Concept sheet, one image: refined decorative "flower objects" that dress a sky citadel town (Halo Citadel: white marble, brushed warm gold, soft sky blue, clouds) in a kid-friendly Roblox RPG. The previous version (ornate pastel, hearts, bows, bubbles, candy gloss) read as too childish — keep it rich and simple, but make it ELEGANT and a bit more grown-up: think garden ornaments of a cathedral courtyard, not toys. Show 8 flower objects in a row like a catalogue, each = a slender stem (white marble or cream with a thin brushed-gold band or two) and a sculpted head built from simple forms: a lantern-like glass orb in a gold calyx, a lily/tulip cup with 3 clean petals and gold edge, a layered lotus of 2 petal rings, a tall slim spire-bud (crystal, pale blue), a bell with a small gold clapper, a stylized rose of stacked rounded petals (ivory + blush), a cloud-blossom (3 soft spheres with one small gold star), a sun-disc with a pearl center. Colors: ivory, blush, soft lavender, pale sky blue, mint, with warm gold accents only — no bright pink, no bows, no floating bubbles, no sparkle bursts, no faces. Restrained soft glow inside the glass heads only. Leaves: 1-2 slim mint leaves with a thin gold vein. Vary heights (one tall centerpiece). Below, smaller: a tall centerpiece in a round white-marble planter with a gold rim, and a row of 5 along a low marble garden edge with low green hedge, so the owner sees them placed in the town. Style: clean stylized 3D render, chunky but smooth forms, satin matte surfaces, NO outlines, NO cast shadows, NO ground shadow, even light-box lighting, plain very-light-cream background, no text, no people. Still buildable from basic parts (cylinders, spheres, cones, discs, rings).
