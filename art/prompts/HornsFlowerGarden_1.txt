One composed concept painting (not a catalogue): a splendid "flower garden corner" for the Horn Legion town in a kid-friendly Roblox RPG — same composition and level of craft as the reference (a tall central hero flower in a round planter, flanked by crystal-cluster flowers of three heights, a thin arch above with two hanging lanterns, a curved low garden edge with hedge on both sides, cloud wisps behind) but in the Horns world: dark volcanic stone and obsidian instead of white marble, brushed iron and dark brass instead of gold, lava-glow accents. Hero flower = a big ember orb (deep orange glowing core inside smoky dark glass) held in a black-iron calyx with curved horn-like tips, with small drifting ember sparks around it. Side flowers = clusters of dark amethyst and ruby crystal shards in iron cups. Stems = dark basalt with thin brass spiral bands, slim leaves in deep teal with a glowing orange vein. Lanterns on the arch = caged ember orbs. Hedge = dark plum foliage with tiny red berries; the planter and garden edge = black stone with a thin lava seam line glowing softly. Mood: dramatic, cool, elegant — a devil's garden that is still cute enough for kids (no gore, no skulls, no faces, no text). Palette: charcoal, obsidian, deep plum, teal-black, with ember orange, ruby and amethyst glow, dark brass. Style: clean stylized 3D render, smooth rounded forms, glass and satin materials, NO outlines, NO cast shadows, NO ground shadow, even light-box lighting, plain very-dark-slate background (not black). Every element buildable from basic parts (spheres, cylinders, cones, rings, discs, wedges).
